# Demo video plan (< 3 minutes, English, YouTube public)

Story: the citizen's emergency call is the most important call most people
will ever make — and it cannot be practiced. Until now.

| Time | Beat | On screen | Judging criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:20 | "In a panic call to 112, everything depends on what the call-taker receives: what happened, where, how urgent. But no citizen can rehearse that call, and no trainer can drill it without a real line. So we made the call testable." | Console hero + a phone | Real-world impact |
| 0:20–0:50 | Preview first: click Preview — the exact task, read the safety lines aloud: self-identifies as an AI demo, never the real 112, directs real emergencies to the real number, never dispatches. | Preview panel JSON | Quality of idea (safety design) |
| 0:50–2:00 | THE CALL: pick Hindi, enter PIN, Start practice call → phone rings on camera → answer → AI opens in Hindi identifying as a demo → reports "meri mummy ko saans nahi aa rahi — Shalimar Bagh" → agent asks one question at a time, confirms location → hang up | Split: console polling + phone | Technical implementation (CALL-E at runtime) |
| 2:00–2:30 | Structured intake lands: emergency "breathing emergency", location "Shalimar Bagh", urgency critical, clarity partial — graded P1 · CRITICAL by local rules that can escalate but never downplay | Result card + history | Completeness |
| 2:30–2:55 | "Practice what you'd say. Measure what they'd hear. The AI never pretends to be the service — it makes the rehearsal possible." | Close on console + repo URL | Close |

## Notes
- One real call in the video (budget it as its own take).
- English VO over the Hindi call audio — the Hindi conversation is the star; don't talk over the self-identification line, let it play 2s.
- Before recording: one warm-up practice call on your own number.
